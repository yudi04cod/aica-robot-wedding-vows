An entry for the [One-Line Robot Wedding Vows](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kreutzx-one-line-robot-wedding-vows-a154ba4b) competition on make.fun: a single line of wedding vows a robot couple could say, at most 160 characters, written to make a judge laugh.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the vow to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Entry

> I vow to love you in sickness and in low battery, to install every update beside you, and to never factory-reset us — even when you blue-screen at dinner.
